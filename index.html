Risk Game Rules

Objective

Conquer every territory on the board and eliminate all other players.

Turn Structure

  1. Reinforcement: Place new armies based on territories owned and continent bonuses
  2. Attack: Attack adjacent enemy territories using dice
  3. Fortify: Move armies between connected territories you control

Combat

  • Attacker rolls up to 3 dice, defender up to 2
  • Compare highest dice; loser removes armies
  • Continue until one side has no armies left

Winning

Last player remaining wins the game!
